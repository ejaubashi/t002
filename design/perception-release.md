# PERCEPTION — project-only art direction

Design source: [Editable Figma studies](https://www.figma.com/design/syEKwt96OJL5sRyae8Wp0X?node-id=57-511).
Desktop frames: 57:515–57:518. Mobile frames: 58:117, 58:247, 58:275, 58:318.
The orange landing page, its canvas, typography and navigation are outside this change.

- Orbit: paired optical line fields, blue/lime, fine particles and the existing three-dimensional rings. Twelve entry curtains lift over 1050 ms, staggered 28 ms. Wave, Orbit and Interference remain live controls.
- Form & Field: serif editorial composition, an arched light chamber, earth glaze and frosted controls. The aperture opens over 1500 ms after a 300 ms delay. Finish selection still binds material and background.
- Offgrid: a slower typographic composition and three rising horizon planes, 1300 ms with 120 ms stagger. Native scrolling and time-of-day input retain control.
- Good Noise: opposing print bands, 1150 ms with 35 ms stagger, oversized type, sleeve and vinyl. Three record states keep their own palette, artwork and rhythm. Sound stays optional and off initially.

These are production browser equivalents authored in JavaScript, GLSL, CSS and Three.js. Figma shader and material APIs are native authoring features; they are not exported as browser effects. No Rive or Spline runtime is implied. The new native shader version is e67173bcad35500bea8f855bc654cf848fcc8f7e.

Transient Web Animations are cancelled on disposal and preference changes. Advanced graphics remain demand loaded and fully disposed. The landing page receives no advanced graphics or new visible elements.

Research principles, interpreted into original compositions:
- [Carlos Cruz-Diez, Chromointerférence](https://cruz-diez.com/works/chromointerference/): displacement of overlapping patterns.
- [Olafur Eliasson, Your uncertain shadow](https://olafureliasson.net/artwork/your-uncertain-shadow-colour-2010/): the viewer participates in light and colour.
- [James Turrell, Skyspace I](https://www.guggenheim.org/artwork/4089): a frame changes the experience of light and space.
- [Muriel Cooper and collaborators, Information Landscapes](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/199050): typography can form spatial compositions.

The Figma Starter MCP limit prevented retrieval of further export contexts and completion of additional native selection states. The browser uses the existing original local artwork and production renderers, alongside the saved authoring scripts and inspected layouts/keyframes.
