# Living Works

Project-only artwork replacement; the orange landing page is preserved.

- **TENSION / Orbit:** an open ruled surface made from lime threads. Wave, fold and interference are three continuous deformations of one drawing. Pointer and keyboard input pull the surface; a pulse briefly opens it.
- **INTERVAL / Form & Field:** one continuous open paper fold, with a fine mineral surface and warm grazing light. Pigment, turning and close inspection replace the product study.
- **DRIFT / Offgrid:** forty suspended ink contours and one continuous horizon thread. Light studies change the atmosphere and the openness of the drawing. Native scrolling adds depth without locking the page.
- **RESONANCE / Good Noise:** three original graphic scores. A folded stroke, a rosette, and angular tension marks each have their own typography, palette, rhythm and movement. Direct turning and reverse artwork controls remain available. Audio starts off.

The pieces are original procedural compositions. Research principles: [Ruth Asawa — economy of line and negative space](https://www.davidzwirner.com/exhibitions/2021/pure-form), [Lygia Clark — participatory forms](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/91767), [Fred Sandback — space defined by line](https://www.nga.gov/artworks/143150-untitled-gray-corner-construction).

Existing Figma palettes, typography and project layouts provide the frame. The account's Starter MCP limit blocked canvas updates during this iteration; these new artworks are authored and validated in the browser rather than claimed as saved Figma changes.

The Vite / JavaScript architecture is preserved. Advanced drawing resources are lazy, paused outside the view, and disposed synchronously on close. Canvas and SVG fallbacks show the same artwork families. New semantic control labels and reduced-motion behavior retain direct keyboard interaction.
